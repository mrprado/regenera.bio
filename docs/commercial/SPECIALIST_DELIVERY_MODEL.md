# Specialist Delivery Model

This document defines what Regenera claims to do, and just as importantly,
what it explicitly does not claim to do. Every service page, sector page,
diagnostic description, and piece of marketing copy should be checkable
against this document. If a sentence implies Regenera performs a licensed
technical service in-house, it fails this standard and needs to be rewritten.

## The operating principle

**Regenera owns the integration layer. Specialists own specialist
conclusions.**

Regenera coordinates across the interfaces between land, infrastructure,
resources, development, ecology, capital, intelligence, and specialist
disciplines. It does not replace the specialists who work within those
disciplines.

## What Regenera does

- systemic diagnosis and place-based analysis
- dependency mapping (see the System Dependency & Feedback methodology,
  structured in the CRM as `project_dependencies`, `docs/crm/DATA_MODEL.md`)
- project readiness assessment
- development strategy and sequencing
- specialist and technical-workstream coordination
- capital readiness review and financing-pathway analysis
- counterparty coordination
- environmental and asset intelligence coordination (including orbital/Earth
  observation data as an input, not as a service Regenera itself operates)
- strategic introductions, where lawful
- monitoring and learning across an engagement

## What Regenera does not claim to do

Regenera does not claim to replace, and should never be described as
functioning as:

- civil, electrical, or mechanical engineers
- EPC contractors
- architects
- hydrologists, ecologists, agronomists, geologists, or mining engineers
- aerospace engineers
- lawyers
- regulated investment professionals (Regenera is not a broker-dealer,
  registered investment adviser, underwriter, placement agent, or fund
  manager, see the site-wide compliance rule in `CLAUDE.md`)
- specialist environmental consultants
- any licensed professional role where the license is a legal requirement to
  perform the work

Where a specific technical, engineering, legal, or geological conclusion is
needed, Regenera coordinates the specialist who is actually qualified to
reach it. Copy should say "coordinates with," "engages," or "works alongside"
qualified specialists, never imply that Regenera's own team produced a
licensed technical conclusion.

## Naming actual partners

Only name a specific partner firm or individual on the public site when that
partner has actually agreed to be named, in the specific context named. Do
not imply an ongoing roster of named specialists that doesn't reflect a real,
current, authorized relationship. Where the site describes the kinds of
disciplines Regenera coordinates with in general terms (engineering, EPC,
architecture, hydrology, ecology, agriculture, geology, legal, project
finance, real estate, Earth observation, aerospace, project management),
that's describing the category of relationship, not claiming a specific
named firm is currently engaged, unless it genuinely is and is authorized to
be named.

## The reader's takeaway

A sophisticated visitor, a project developer, an EPC executive, a mining or
resource counterparty, should leave a Regenera page thinking: *Regenera is
not the engineer, lender, architect, developer, fund, mining engineer, or
aerospace company. It works across those interfaces and helps a complex
project become coherent enough for the right specialists, counterparties,
and capital to work together.* If a page doesn't produce that read, or reads
as though Regenera itself performs the specialist work, it needs to be
rewritten before it goes live.

## Applying this alongside the Regenerative Claims Standard

This document and `REGENERATIVE_CLAIMS_STANDARD.md` work together: one keeps
Regenera from overclaiming *what it does*, the other keeps it from
overclaiming *what an intervention accomplishes*. A page can pass one check
and fail the other, review copy against both before publishing.

## Recommended Services-page integration statement (use verbatim)

The Master Operating Blueprint (section 38) specifies this language for the
Services page (and Approach/About where relevant):

> "One point of coordination across a multidisciplinary project. Regenera
> does not replace the engineers, scientists, designers, counsel, financial
> professionals, or other specialists a project requires. We work across
> those disciplines: defining the system, identifying dependencies and
> constraints, sequencing the work, coordinating counterparties, and
> translating findings into development and capital decisions."

## Per-discipline relationship table (section 37)

For technical sector pages that need to state the boundary explicitly
rather than only in general terms:

| Discipline | Regenera's relationship |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering / EPC | Frames readiness, coordinates interfaces, supports owner-side decisions; licensed engineers perform design and certification. |
| Hydrology / environmental science | Integrates findings into project decisions; qualified specialists perform discipline-specific studies. |
| Geology / mine engineering | Analyzes resource, land, water, energy, logistics, and capital context; qualified professionals perform reserves, mine design, geotechnical work, and technical sign-off. |
| Architecture / planning | Coordinates system requirements and development strategy; architects/planners produce discipline-specific design and statutory work. |
| Legal / permitting | Identifies legal/regulatory dependencies and coordinates workstreams; counsel provides legal advice. |
| Investment decisions | Supports readiness, capital strategy, introductions, and context; investors make independent investment decisions. |
| Aerospace engineering | Assesses terrestrial systems, resource dependencies, environmental context, and orbital-economy interfaces; aerospace specialists handle engineering. |

## The desired first-impression sequence (section 38)

The site should be designed so a sophisticated first-time visitor reaches
these conclusions, roughly in this order: this is a sophisticated
systems-oriented advisory platform; I understand exactly what problem they
solve for someone like me; they are not pretending to replace the engineer,
lawyer, fund manager, ecologist, or specialist; their methodology is
repeatable, not merely philosophical; their regenerative claims are
evidence-based rather than marketing language; their project examples and
claims are clearly distinguished and supportable; there is an obvious next
step. Use this as a review checklist against any new page, not just a
description.
