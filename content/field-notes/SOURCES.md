# Field Notes Source Watchlist

Organized by desk, matching the 8 categories in `lib/fieldNotesTaxonomy.ts`.
See `EDITORIAL_SYSTEM.md` for the tier system (primary / institutional /
trade media / discovery-only) these sources sit in. Discovery-only sources
(general news, newsletters, social, aggregators) are omitted here on
purpose, use them to find stories, never to source a figure.

## Capital Markets & Real Assets
World Bank · IFC · EIB · EBRD · IDB Invest · AfDB · Green Climate Fund ·
national development finance institutions · pension funds · infrastructure
investors · Infrastructure Investor · New Private Markets · Carbon Pulse ·
UNEP · CBD (Convention on Biological Diversity) · Forest Trends · TNFD ·
PRI · The Nature Conservancy · Carbon Brief (for natural capital and
environmental-markets stories specifically)
